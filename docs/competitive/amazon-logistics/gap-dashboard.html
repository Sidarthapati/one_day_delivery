The gaps, in detail

Expand each theme. Verdict reflects the whole cluster.

▸ Exceptions, problem-solve & RTO Missing

Amazon Exceptions view + manage-packages: reschedule windows, change assigned station, on-road override, batch tracking-ID ops, a real reason-code taxonomy, and disposition rollups (reattemptable / undeliverable / missing / returned-to-station).

Us State-machine states exist for failed/RTO/reschedule and a station /exceptions page — but M11 is an empty shell: nothing emits the exception event, so there's no engine, no reason taxonomy (failures are free-text), no attempt policy, no call-center queue, no batch tooling.

Effort · High A whole module. The single highest-leverage gap.

▸ Ops analytics & reporting Partial

Amazon Per-DA pace & projected-RTS dashboards, attempt-success %, GPS-trace replay with a time-scrubber, ageing buckets by days-in-status, DA scorecards, and CSV/Excel export everywhere.

Us We store the raw material — da_gps_ping, dispatch queue, scan_ledger, SLA legs — and have SLA lanes + a live van map. Missing are the aggregations: pace/RTS/attempt-success, trace replay, ageing report, scorecards, and export.

Effort · Medium Mostly a Station-console dashboard over data we already have.

▸ Package search & event timeline Partial

Amazon Global package search (rich filters, 1000+, CSV, shareable deep-links) → per-package event timeline with actor, node, driver and GPS.

Us A strong, tamper-proof spine — append-only scan_ledger + shipment_state_history + custody + handoff. What's missing is a unified ops "search → full timeline" view, plus export and deep-links.

Effort · Low–Medium UI over existing data + an export endpoint.

▸ COD, cash & deposits Have Partial

Have Driver cash reconciliation — DA deposits, admin cash-recon, B2B remittance (gross/fee/net/UTR), bank penny-drop — good parity.

Partial Amazon adds an expected-vs-actual cash variance loop and reconciles against physical barcoded deposit slips scanned back. We have no variance workflow or cash-management-services integration.

Effort · Medium

▸ Workforce: schedule · performance · pay Missing

Amazon Scheduling + shift planning (work blocks), DA performance scorecards, and a DA/DSP Payments console.

Us Roster + shift jobs exist (G1 shipped), but no shift-planning UI, no performance scorecards at all, and no driver earnings/pay (our "payout" is COD only).

Effort Performance = medium (derive from ledger); pay = high (new domain).

▸ Partner loss-recovery & disputes Missing

Amazon A dedicated chargeback tool: per-parcel ₹ loss, loss buckets, recoverable classification, SLA-bound dispute lifecycle, bulk dispute/export.

Us Nothing comparable — and it matters for our B2B + own-fleet model once partner accountability and loss attribution scale.

Effort · High A new module.

▸ Fraud / integrity & data richness Missing Partial

Fraud · Missing Amazon tracks fake-GPS (spoofing), bad-scan analytics and deductions. Our append-only ledger is a good base, but no spoof detection or bad-scan analytics exist.

Data · Partial We have core weight/dims/volumetric/payment/SLA/ETA. Thinner: ship-speed class, sort zone/cluster/aisle, dwell-per-scan, service-type taxonomy, promised-vs-RDD split, merchant attribution.

Effort Fraud = medium (analytics over GPS+scans); data = low–medium (additive columns).

Close the gap — in this order

Highest leverage first; steps 1–2 reuse data we already store.

Build the M11 exceptions / problem-solve engine

Reason-code taxonomy, attempt policy, RTO producer, reschedule / reassign / on-road override, batch ops, disposition rollups. Unblocks the biggest single delta.

Effort · High

Ship the ops-analytics layer

Per-DA pace / RTS / attempt-success dashboard, GPS-trace replay, ageing report, DA scorecards, and CSV export across consoles. Mostly aggregation over existing data.

Effort · Medium

Cash variance + deposit scan-back, then DA/DSP payments

Expected-vs-actual reconciliation with barcoded slips; then a real driver-earnings ledger.

Effort · Medium → High

Partner loss-recovery module + fraud analytics

Chargeback/dispute workflow and fake-GPS / bad-scan monitoring over the ledger.

Effort · High → Medium

Enrich the parcel data model

Ship-class, sort granularity, dwell, service-type, promised-vs-RDD — folded into steps 1–2.

Effort · Low–Medium

Where we're already ahead

  • On-road driver app. Our native app (pickup/delivery + failure sheets, van load/loop/stops/return-scan, shuttle, camera barcode scan, background GPS, native-maps nav, OTP proof) is a real product. Amazon's driver app wasn't even shown.
  • Append-only integrity. A DB-trigger-enforced immutable scan ledger is a strong foundation for audit and fraud work.
  • Intercity + air leg. Our airline (M9), hub bags and shuttle (M12) model a multi-leg network richer than a single-station last-mile view.
  • Shipper self-serve. Customer web + B2B console (wallet, dev keys/webhooks, white-label tracking, bulk upload, invoices, remittances) — a surface Amazon's ops video doesn't touch.
